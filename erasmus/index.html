Curriculum Structure 5 Core Pillars

Class 4B INF: Telecommunications Pillars

The 4th year is the pivotal transition where theoretical physics and mathematical analysis converge into real-world communication hardware, high-frequency lines, and protocols.

01
Signal Theory

Deterministic vs stochastic signals. Continuous, sampled, and digital waveforms. Peak, RMS, average parameters with live oscilloscope controls.

02
Fourier Spectral Analysis

Fourier series decomposition of periodic composite waves into sinusoidal harmonics. Harmonic spectrum, bandwidth, and live waveform synthesis.

03
Transmission Media

Guided media (twisted pair, coax, Single-Mode and Multi-Mode optical fibers) with interactive Snell's Law and total internal reflection simulator.

04
Modulation Systems

Analog (AM, FM) and digital shift keying (ASK, FSK) with real-time waveform modulation sliders and Nyquist-Shannon sampling visualization.

05
Protocols & Architecture

OSI 7-layer and TCP/IP models, Ethernet framing, VLANs, and digital oscilloscope "Rule of 5" bandwidth calibration simulator.

🔬
Hands-on Laboratory

40% of class time is spent on bench testing with digital storage oscilloscopes (DSOs), arbitrary waveform generators, and Cisco Packet Tracer.

Mathematical Formulation

$$\displaystyle s(t) = A_0 \cos(2\pi f_0 t) + V_{offset}$$

  • Peak Voltage $V_p$: Maximum excursion from baseline ($|A_0|$).
  • Peak-to-Peak $V_{pp}$: $2 \cdot A_0$ (total peak span).
  • DC Average $V_{avg}$: Equals offset voltage ($V_{offset}$).
  • RMS Voltage $V_{rms}$: $\sqrt{V_{offset}^2 + A_0^2/2}$ (effective thermal work).

Module 2 🎛️ Live Harmonic Synthesizer

Fourier Spectral Synthesis in Real-Time

Jean-Baptiste Fourier's theorem states that any periodic wave can be reconstructed as a sum of sinusoidal harmonics. Test it live below!

𝝨
Fourier Decomposition

$$\displaystyle s(t) = a_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left[ a_n \cos(n \omega_0 t) + b_n \sin(n \omega_0 t) \right]$$

  • Fundamental: $f_0 = 1/T$ (first harmonic, sets fundamental tone).
  • Harmonics: Integer multiples $f_n = n \cdot f_0$.
  • Square wave: Only odd harmonics ($1f, 3f, 5f\dots$) with amplitude $1/n$.
  • Bandwidth $B$: Channels must transmit up to harmonic $N$ to preserve rise-time!

Module 3 🎛️ Optical Waveguide Simulator

Transmission Media & Snell's Law Physics

Experience total internal reflection (TIR) inside glass fiber. Adjust angle $\theta$ and refractive index $n_1$ to see the ray propagate or leak into cladding!

📐
Snell's Law & Critical Angle ($\theta_c$)

$$\displaystyle n_{core} > n_{cladding} \implies \theta_c = \arcsin\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)$$

  • $\theta \ge \theta_c$: Total Internal Reflection (TIR)
    100% of light is reflected back into the core with zero boundary leakage.
  • $\theta < \theta_c$: Refraction / Escaping Ray
    Light refracts into cladding ($n_2$), causing rapid optical signal death.
  • Single-Mode ($9\,\mu\text{m}$): Eliminates intermodal dispersion.

Module 4 🎛️ Live Carrier Modulation Simulator

Modulation Systems: AM, FM, ASK & FSK

Modulating a high-frequency carrier wave with audio or digital telemetry. Toggle AM, FM, ASK, or FSK and adjust carrier frequency in real time!

📡
Why We Modulate Radio Waves
  • Antenna length formula: $\ell \approx \lambda/4 = c / (4f)$. A $1\,\text{kHz}$ voice signal needs a $75\,\text{km}$ antenna! Transmitted at $100\,\text{MHz}$, $\ell = 75\,\text{cm}$.
  • AM (Amplitude): $s(t) = [A_c + m(t)] \cos(2\pi f_c t)$, bandwidth $B_{AM} = 2f_m$.
  • FM (Frequency): Carson's rule $B_{FM} \approx 2(\Delta f + f_m)$ with superior noise immunity.
  • ASK / FSK: Digital binary shift keying for data telemetry.
Bandwidth formula: B = 2 · f_m

Module 5 🎛️ DSO Bandwidth Simulator

Lab Instrumentation & Bandwidth "Rule of 5"

See how an oscilloscope's analog bandwidth impacts measurement accuracy! If $B_{scope} < 5 \times f_{signal}$, high harmonics are lost and edges turn round!

🎯
The DSO Bandwidth Criterion
Bscope ≥ 5 × fsignal
  • Harmonic capture: 5th harmonic captures 98% of signal energy.
  • Rise time relation: $t_r \approx 0.35 / B_{scope}$.
  • Rule violation: Low bandwidth attenuates high harmonics, transforming digital pulses into slow sinusoids!
Ratio Bscope / fsig: 5.0x
